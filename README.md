# Expedition 51: Trans-Labrador & Atlantic ADV Motorcycle Loop
### 18 Days • June 16 – Saturday, July 3, 2027 • 100% Domestic Canadian Route • ~7,854 km

Master expedition itinerary and navigation package for long-distance motorcycle adventure through Quebec's Côte-Nord, the Trans-Labrador Highway (TLH 500 / 510), Western Newfoundland's Viking Trail, Cape Breton's Cabot Trail, Prince Edward Island, and the Bay of Fundy.

---

## Tour Highlights & Strategy (2027 Calendar)
* **Saturday Completion Advantage**: Tour departs **Wednesday, June 16, 2027** and concludes **Saturday, July 3, 2027**, allowing Sunday, July 4, 2027 as a dedicated recovery day before returning to regular weekday schedules.
* **Outbound via Pembroke, Portage-du-Fort, Route 148 & Mirabel**: Follows Highway 17 to Pembroke, crosses the Ottawa River into Quebec at Portage-du-Fort via the Chenaux Generating Station dam, and proceeds along Route 148 through Shawville, Quyon, Aylmer, Gatineau (Boulevard des Allumettières), Montebello, and Lachute to Mirabel for Night 1. Day 2 continues east via Route 158 and historic Chemin du Roy (Route 138) along the St. Lawrence North Shore through Trois-Rivières, Quebec City, Charlevoix, and Tadoussac to Baie-Comeau.
* **Direct TLH 510 Mainline**: Follows the continuous Route 510 mainline south through the Mealy Mountains corridor to Port Hope Simpson and Blanc-Sablon.
* **Night 10 Marine Atlantic Overnight Crossing**: Private en-suite 2-berth stateroom aboard the 23:45 overnight ferry from Port aux Basques, arriving at 07:00 AM Saturday into North Sydney for an early morning start on the Cabot Trail.
* **Two-Day Cabot Trail**: Day 11 traverses Cape Smokey, Neil's Harbour Lighthouse, and Broad Cove Campground. Day 12 covers the Meat Cove clifftop coastal spur, French Mountain, and Chéticamp Campground.
* **PEI Grand Island Loop & Cavendish Beach**: Enters PEI via the Confederation Bridge (Day 13), visiting Victoria-by-the-Sea, Cape Tryon Lighthouse, and PEI National Park, camping at Cavendish Beach. Day 14 departs via Seacow Head Lighthouse, Cape Enrage, and Hopewell Rocks to Fundy National Park.
* **Consolidated Attractions Category (23 POIs)**: Heritage and cultural points of interest are organized into the unified **"Attractions" (23 POIs)** category, contributing to **61 Curated Gems** (28 Lighthouses • 23 Attractions • 10 Short Hikes).
* **18-Day Master Saddle Timeline & Gantt Chart**: Integrated interactive timeline Gantt chart visualizing daily operational schedules (07:00 AM standard rollout, 06:00 AM ferry wakeups, 30m meal breaks, 1h supper, saddle intervals, and fuel stops) with interactive tooltips and day focusing.
* **Comprehensive 28-Lighthouse Maritime Catalog & 72 Gas Stations**: Full database of 203 POIs including 28 historic coastal lighthouses, 72 fuel stations (with 91 AKI availability tags and backup options), 23 attractions, 10 short hikes, 19 primary lodgings, 18 fallback hotels, 14 parks, 7 ferries/bridges, and 12 scenic sights.
* **Three-Day Return Leg (~431 km Daily Average)**: The return leg from Edmundston is divided into three manageable days: Day 16 to Drummondville (437 km) via Highway 20, Day 17 to Pembroke via Highway 30 and Highway 148 (502 km), and Day 18 to Hanmer Home Base (355 km).
* **Lodging Distribution**: 1 service-equipped KOA (Gros Morne), 8 Provincial & National Parks, 2 oceanfront cabins (L'Anse aux Meadows), 1 Marine Atlantic private stateroom, and 5 commercial hotels/motels.
* **Estimated Dual-Rider Budget**: ~$5,450 CAD total (~$2,450 savings relative to an all-hotel itinerary).
