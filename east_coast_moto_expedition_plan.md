## Core Operational Strategy
1. **100% Domestic Canadian Route**: No passports or US customs checkpoints required.
2. **Saturday Homecoming Advantage**: Returning on **Saturday, July 3, 2027** provides an entire Sunday (July 4) to relax, unpack, wash gear, and decompress before returning to work Monday.
3. **Outbound via Pembroke, Portage-du-Fort, Route 148 & Mirabel Overnight**:
   - Follow Trans-Canada Hwy 17 east from Hanmer past North Bay to Pembroke.
   - At Pembroke / Haley Station, cross the Ottawa River via Chenaux Generating Station dam into Portage-du-Fort, QC onto Route 148 East in the Pontiac region.
   - Stay strictly on Route 148 East through Shawville, Quyon, and Aylmer.
   - Skirt Gatineau via Boulevard des Allumettières (QC-148) and Boulevard Maloney Est staying strictly on Route 148 (Chemin de Montréal Ouest through Templeton-Est and Angers, Pont Major across Rivière du Lièvre, and Chemin de Montréal Est through Masson-Angers), completely avoiding Autoroute 50.
   - Follow scenic Highway 148 along the river through Thurso, Plaisance, Papineauville, Montebello, Fassett, Calumet, and Grenville directly to Saint-Philippe-d'Argenteuil.
   - Follow Rue Water into Lachute, then take Route 158 through Saint-Canut and north onto Boulevard du Curé-Labelle (Route 117) directly into Mirabel, QC to **Motel au P'tit Sapin** (16033 Bd Curé-Labelle).
   - Overnight at Motel au P'tit Sapin, enjoying room-front exterior motorcycle parking and zero city congestion.
   - On Day 2, take Boulevard Curé-Labelle north into Saint-Jérôme, continuing east along Route 158 through Joliette to Berthierville, joining historic Chemin du Roy (Route 138) through Trois-Rivières to Quebec City, Côte-de-Beaupré, Charlevoix mountains (Route 362), Tadoussac marine ferry, and Baie-Comeau.
   - **100% North Shore of the St. Lawrence River; uses zero meters of Highway 40, avoids Ottawa traffic, and bypasses Montreal Island by 40+ km!**
4. **Direct TLH 510 Mainline (Zero Paradise River Detour)**:
   - Day 5 stays strictly on the direct, 100% paved mainline Trans-Labrador Highway (TLH 510) through Port Hope Simpson straight south to Blanc-Sablon. All northern loop spurs toward Paradise River (`lat > 53.30 N`) are 100% purged.
5. **Night 10 Overnight Marine Atlantic Ferry Stateroom & 07:00 AM Jump**:
   - Eliminates North Sydney KOA completely. Instead of arriving in North Sydney in the evening only to sleep at a campsite 8 minutes from the dock, ride the Codroy Valley and visit Cape Anguille and Cape Ray Lighthouses, board the 23:45 overnight ferry in Port aux Basques, and sleep in a private 2-berth en-suite stateroom cabin.
   - Disembark at 07:00 AM Saturday morning into North Sydney, fully rested and fed, to hit the Cabot Trail East ahead of all tourist traffic.
6. **Split Cabot Trail with Oceanfront Broad Cove Camp**:
   - *Part 1 (Sat Jun 26)*: North Sydney 7 AM rollout $\rightarrow$ Baddeck $\rightarrow$ Cape Smokey hairpins $\rightarrow$ Neil's Harbour Lighthouse $\rightarrow$ Broad Cove Campground (Cape Breton Highlands NP) in Ingonish Beach.
   - *Part 2 (Sun Jun 27)*: Broad Cove $\rightarrow$ Meat Cove clifftop adventure spur $\rightarrow$ French Mountain hairpins $\rightarrow$ Skyline Trail hike $\rightarrow$ Chéticamp Campground.
7. **PEI Grand Island Loop & Oceanfront Cavendish Beach Camping**:
   - Cross the 12.9 km Confederation Bridge onto PEI (100% toll-free inbound).
   - Ride the PEI Grand Island Loop: Victoria-by-the-Sea & 1879 Range Light, French River look-off, Cape Tryon Lighthouse (110-ft red cliffs, Montgomery's Four Winds Light), Anne of Green Gables Heritage Place, North Rustico Harbour pepperpot light, and Covehead dunes.
   - Camp oceanfront at **Cavendish Campground** inside Prince Edward Island National Park, nestled directly behind the red sandstone cliffs and sandy beach.
   - Day 14 departs via the 1863 octagonal Seacow Head Lighthouse, crosses Confederation Bridge back to New Brunswick, and carves Route 915 to Cape Enrage Lighthouse & 150-ft cliffs and Hopewell Rocks.
8. **Comprehensive 28-Lighthouse Maritime Catalog & St. Anthony**:
   - All 28 historic, prominent coastal lighthouses across Quebec, Labrador, Newfoundland (including **Fishing Point Lighthouse in St. Anthony!**), Nova Scotia, PEI, and New Brunswick are fully mapped with precise GPS coordinates, Garmin-compatible `<sym>Light</sym>` waypoint tags, and interactive map filters!
   - Stage 6 explicitly visits **St. Anthony**, touring the **Grenfell Historic Properties & Museum**, hiking the ocean cliff trails at **Fishing Point Lighthouse (Fox Point Light)**, and refueling with 91 Premium before retiring to Snorri Cabins in Hay Cove for the Summer Solstice.
9. **3-Stage Return Pacing (<450 km Daily Average)**:
   - Stage 16 (Canada Day): Edmundston to Drummondville (437 km) along the St. Lawrence South Shore staying strictly on Highway 20 (Autoroute Jean-Lesage) past Rivière-du-Loup, Montmagny, and south of Lévis/Charny with zero ferry or city detours. Stay at Camping des Voltigeurs.
   - Stage 17: Drummondville to Pembroke (502 km) via A-30 Montreal South Shore bypass over Pont Serge-Marcil toll bridge, and southern Ottawa rural bypass (Mitch Owens Rd through Manotick and Carleton Place). Stay at Best Western Pembroke Inn with indoor pool and hot tub.
   - Stage 18 (Saturday Homecoming): Pembroke to Hanmer Home Base (355 km) via Hwy 17 through Mattawa and North Bay.

    - **1 Strategic Recovery KOA**: Gros Morne KOA (Day 8) for laundry, heated pool, and motorcycle wash.
    - **8 Scenic National & Provincial Parks**: Green Point NP (Day 9), Broad Cove NP (Day 11), Chéticamp NP (Day 12), Cavendish PEI NP (Day 13), Fundy NP (Day 14), de la République PP (Day 15), and Camping des Voltigeurs Sépaq (Day 16).
